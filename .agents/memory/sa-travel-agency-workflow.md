name: SA Travel agency workflow
description: Product context for the flight agency workspace and its customer travel services.
---

SA Travel is for a flight agency that helps customers arrange flights and ground transfers, including pickups and drop-offs. Live flight monitoring belongs in the same staff workspace as customer itineraries and transfer coordination, with one staff login.

**Why:** the user clarified that the system is for an agency serving customers who request flights and transport, not a standalone flight tracker or separate CRM.

**How to apply:** keep booking, fleet-transfer, and flight-monitoring workflows together in the existing SA Travel web app; treat public customer contact and booking intake as a distinct optional next step, not as a second staff application.
